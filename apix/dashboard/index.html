About the Airfare Consumer Price Index (CPI)

Official prototype explaining the economic rationale, econometric de-biasing, and Törnqvist index formulation behind Airiva. Designed for statistical integration with the Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation (MoSPI) and Reserve Bank of India (RBI).

1. Why an Airfare Price Index?

Unlike standard consumer goods with sticky prices, airline tickets change price dynamically by the minute based on capacity algorithms, booking horizons, and competitor actions. Merely averaging posted web fares on arbitrary dates produces extreme noise and introduces severe sampling bias.

Airiva constructs a pure price index that isolates actual tariff inflation from passenger mix shifts, booking window distortions, and carrier seat bucket variations.

2. Why the Törnqvist Formulation?

Traditional price indexes like Laspeyres or Paasche suffer from substitution bias when consumers shift toward cheaper booking windows or carriers. The Törnqvist index is a superlative index number formula that provides a second-order approximation to any arbitrary expenditure aggregator function.

TÖRNQVIST LOGARITHMIC INDEX FORMULA:
ln(Pt / P0) = ∑i [ (wi,0 + wi,t) / 2 ] × ln( pi,t / pi,0 )
Where pi,t is the median price in corridor-window cell i, and wi is the expenditure weight.

3. Why DGCA Table 4.3 Weights?

To reflect true consumer outlays, Airiva weights corridor sub-indices using verified annual city-pair passenger volumes published in Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) Table 4.3. This guarantees that mega-corridors like Delhi–Mumbai carry proportional macroeconomic significance over thinner regional routes.

4. Econometric De-Biasing & Right-Censoring

When a flight sells out, traditional scrapers drop the row, causing survivor bias (since only high-fare or low-fare seats may remain). Airiva retains sold-out flights as right-censored observations (styled at 45% opacity in microdata tables), applies IQR outlier filtering across corridor-window cells, and deduplicates identical quotes between airline direct feeds and online travel agencies (OTAs).

Sampling Strategy

• Daily Frequency: Nightly automated collection at 06:00 IST to capture yield resets.

• Dual Windows: T+7 (business/short-lead) and T+30 (advance baseline) fixed ratio.

• Deduplication: Direct airline quotes prioritized over OTA quotes to avoid commission double-counting.
